Privacy Policy

Short version: Fleet runs on your Mac and keeps your work there. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends your code, your conversations or your terminal output anywhere.

This policy covers the Fleet app for macOS and this website, fleetapp.dev. Fleet is made by David Krasilshik, who is responsible for both (“we” below).

What stays on your Mac

To do its job, Fleet works with files on your own computer. None of it leaves your Mac.

  • Claude Code conversations. Fleet reads Claude Code’s session files in~/.claude/projects to list conversations you can resume, to name them, and to show the usage dashboard.
  • Setup. On launch Fleet installs a status hook in~/.claude/hooks, adds it to ~/.claude/settings.json, and adds a marked block to ~/.tmux.conf. It keeps agent status, and a short log of the last few hundred status events, in ~/.cache/fleet.
  • Your settings and layout, such as agent names, groups, windows and shortcuts, are stored in Fleet’s preferences on your Mac.
  • Notifications are shown by macOS on your Mac; they don’t go through any server.

What Fleet sends over the internet

Fleet itself connects to the internet only to keep itself up to date:

  • Update checks. Fleet checks fleetapp.dev/appcast.xml for new versions, and fleetapp.dev/policy.json to learn if a version must be updated. These requests carry the app’s name and version, and nothing about you or your work.
  • Downloads. When you install an update, it is downloaded from GitHub.

Like any web request, these reveal your IP address to the server. fleetapp.dev and the downloads are hosted by GitHub, which keeps standard server logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don’t receive or keep those logs.

Problem reports

If you choose Help ▸ Report a Problem, Fleet gathers what it saw: each agent's status details, the last few hundred status events, version numbers, and Fleet's own entries in your Claude Code and tmux settings. Before anything is sent you can read all of it and hide agent and project names. Fleet then opens an email in your own mail app, addressed to us; it's sent only if you send it. Reports never include your terminal, your conversations or your files. We use them only to fix the problem you reported, and delete them when we're done.

This website

fleetapp.dev sets no cookies, runs no analytics or ads, and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. Your browser talks only to GitHub, which hosts the site.

Other services you use with Fleet

  • Claude Code is made by Anthropic. The agents you run in Fleet are Claude Code itself, signed in with your own Anthropic account, and what they send to Anthropic is covered by Anthropic’s privacy policy. Fleet doesn’t see or relay that traffic.
  • Homebrew, if you install Fleet with it, has its own analytics policy.

Your choices

Because we don’t hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. Uninstalling Fleet and removing the files listed above removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Fleet ever starts collecting anything new, this page will say so before it does.
